## Google's "Who / How / Why" Test

| Question | What to look for |
|---|---|
| **Who** created it? | Visible byline, author bio, professional credentials |
| **How** was it created? | Process disclosure, original research, first-hand evidence |
| **Why** does it exist? | To help people, not just to attract search clicks |

## E-E-A-T Framework

### Experience
- Original research, case studies, before/after results
- Personal anecdotes, process documentation
- Unique data, proprietary insights

### Expertise
- Author credentials, certifications, bio
- Technical depth appropriate for audience
- Accurate, well-sourced claims

### Authoritativeness
- External citations, backlinks from authoritative sources
- Brand mentions, industry recognition

### Trustworthiness
- Contact information, privacy policy, terms of service
- Customer testimonials, date stamps
- Secure site (HTTPS)

## Word Count Minimums (topical coverage floors, not targets)

| Page Type | Minimum |
|-----------|---------|
| Homepage | 500 |
| Service page | 800 |
| Blog post | 1,500 |
| Product page | 300+ |
| Location page | 500–600 |

> Word count is NOT a direct ranking factor. Comprehensive topical coverage matters, not word count.

## Keyword Optimization

- Primary keyword in title, H1, first 100 words
- Natural density (1–3%)
- Semantic variations present
- No keyword stuffing

## AI Content Assessment

**Acceptable**: Demonstrates genuine E-E-A-T, unique value, human oversight, original insights.

**Low-Quality markers**: Generic phrasing, no original insight, repetitive structure, no author attribution, factual inaccuracies.

## AI Citation Readiness (GEO)

- Clear, quotable statements with statistics/facts
- Structured data for key data points
- Strong heading hierarchy (H1→H2→H3)
- Answer-first formatting for key questions
- Tables and lists for comparative data
- Monitor: Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Bing Copilot
