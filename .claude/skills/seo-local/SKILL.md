# seo-local

Local SEO analysis — Google Business Profile optimization, citation health, review signals, and location-specific ranking factors.

## Scoring (0-100)

| Factor | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| GBP Signals | 25% |
| Reviews & Reputation | 20% |
| Local On-Page SEO | 20% |
| NAP Consistency | 15% |
| Schema Markup | 10% |
| Local Link Authority | 10% |

## Business Type Detection

- **Brick-and-mortar**: physical address visible
- **Service area businesses (SABs)**: no street address, service radius language
- **Hybrid**: both address and service area

## Industry Verticals

Recognizes: restaurants, healthcare, legal, home services, real estate, automotive → routes to specialized recommendation sets.

## Critical Local SEO Insights

- Dedicated service pages = #1 local organic factor AND #2 AI visibility factor
- **18-day rule**: ranking drops if no new reviews appear within 3 weeks
- Primary GBP category accuracy = single most important local pack factor
- 3 of top 5 AI visibility factors are citation-related

## AI Search for Local

- ChatGPT conversion rates: 15.9% vs 1.76% Google organic
- ChatGPT does NOT access GBP directly — sources from Bing index, Yelp, TripAdvisor, BBB, Reddit

## Quick Wins

- Claim Apple Business Connect and Bing Places
- Fix NAP inconsistencies
- Add proper schema markup
- Target "best of" list placements
- Create location-specific, non-interchangeable content
