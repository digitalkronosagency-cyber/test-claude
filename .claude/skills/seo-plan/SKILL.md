# seo-plan

Strategic SEO planning — 6-phase implementation framework with 12-month roadmap.

**License:** MIT | **Version:** 2.0.0 | **Author:** AgriciDaniel

## 6 Planning Phases

1. Discovery
2. Competitive analysis
3. Architecture design
4. Content strategy
5. Technical foundation
6. Phased 12-month roadmap

## Industry Templates

- SaaS
- Local services
- E-commerce
- Publishing
- Agencies
- General business

1. Strategy document

4. Content calendar
5. Technical checklist
